🪙 Draw coins, not just notes

The National Park confirms card payment fails at some car parks with no internet. New Dungeon Ghyll has no phone-pay at all — the signal is dead. Bring ~£20 in £1 coins. This is the single most-missed Lakes detail and you now have a car.

📵 The valleys are blackspots

Kirkstone, Honister, Wrynose, Hardknott and essentially every fell path are documented dead zones — the fells screen signal even close to town. Never schedule a call on Saturday or Sunday. Those are the mountain days.
